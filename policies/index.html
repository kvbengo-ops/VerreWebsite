Customer policies

Effective 8 August 2026

These policies describe how the current Verre website and order-request process work. Confirm the final commercial terms in your written quote before paying.

Privacy notice

Verre collects the information you choose to send—such as your name, email, phone number, delivery address, commission brief and reference photos—to answer enquiries, prepare quotes, fulfil confirmed orders and provide order updates.

Order and account records are stored in Verre’s private business database. Reference photos are stored privately and are available only through protected studio tools. Email delivery and file-storage providers process the minimum information needed to provide those services. Verre does not sell customer information.

The browser saves only cart contents and non-sensitive commission choices. Names, contact details, addresses and free-text briefs are not kept as local drafts. To ask for access, correction or deletion, email hello@verrecrafts.ph. Some transaction records may need to be retained for accounting, dispute or legal obligations.

Orders and payment

Submitting the website form sends an order or commission request; it does not charge you and does not by itself create a confirmed sale. Verre will reply with availability, the final price, any delivery charge, timing and payment instructions. A custom-work estimate shown by the wizard is a guide only; the written quote is the price that applies.

Shipping and pickup

Available methods include Cebu pickup, Cebu delivery and nationwide shipping. The quote will identify the method, charge and expected dispatch window. Handmade lead times begin only after the quote is accepted and any requested payment is received. Carrier delays after dispatch may be outside Verre’s control, but the studio will help trace a delayed parcel.

Returns, damage and cancellations

Please report an incorrect or damaged item promptly, with the order reference and clear photos, so Verre can assess a repair, replacement or refund. Custom and personalized work normally cannot be returned for a change of mind after production begins. Any cancellation window, deposit treatment and item-specific exception will be stated in the written quote. These terms do not limit rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Retention and security

Verre keeps active order records for fulfilment and support, then retains only what is reasonably needed for accounting, legal obligations and resolving disputes. Unattached photo uploads are deleted automatically when attachment fails; attached reference photos can be deleted on request when they are no longer needed for an active order or legal record.
