The maker

Hi, I'm the hands
behind Verre

Verre started on a tiny desk in Cebu City with one glass jar, a set of secondhand brushes, and far too many beads. “Verre” is French for glass — the little surface where every piece begins.

Today it's still just me: painting, stringing charms, and cutting stickers by hand between weekend markets and artist alleys around the city.
